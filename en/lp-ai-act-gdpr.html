EU AI Act & GDPR

From December 2, 2026, transparency obligations apply to AI systems already in use: are you ready?

  • Fines of up to €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover for the most serious violations.These are the ceilings set for the most serious violations by large companies: the EU AI Act requires authorities to apply proportionate penalties, also taking into account the size and economic interests of SMEs and startups.
  • Anyone interacting with your chatbot or AI agent must know it: disclosure to customers is no longer optional.
  • You need a documented register of which AI agents you use, on what data, and under what human oversight.
