Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Data Controller

GTAVIANI Consulting — Milan (Italy) — VAT number: IT12220360965 — is the data controller for the personal data collected through the aiagents.gtaviani.com website. For any request concerning the processing of your personal data, you can write to privacy (at) gtaviani.com.

Data collected and purposes of processing

The website collects browsing data in aggregate form (pages visited, traffic source, device) through Google Analytics, installed via Google Tag Manager. This data is used to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content.

If you fill in one of the contact forms on the website, we collect the data you voluntarily provide (first and last name, email address, phone number, role within the company). The forms are managed through the third-party provider Tally (Tally Labs, Inc.), which processes this data on our behalf as a data processor. We use this data exclusively to contact you regarding your consulting request, on the basis of your consent and for the performance of pre-contractual measures requested by you. We retain the data collected through the forms for the time necessary to handle your request and, should a consulting relationship be established, for the duration of that relationship and within the terms required by law; you can request its deletion at any time by writing to privacy (at) gtaviani.com.

Cookies and advertising platforms

In addition to the technical cookies necessary for the website to function, we use third-party cookies for statistical and advertising purposes, through the following platforms:

  • Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager — statistical traffic analysis
  • Google Ads — measurement and remarketing of advertising campaigns on the Google network
  • LinkedIn Ads — measurement and remarketing of advertising campaigns on LinkedIn
  • Meta Ads — measurement and remarketing of advertising campaigns on Facebook and Instagram

These services may set cookies or similar technologies on your device to recognize you on subsequent visits and show you relevant ads on other websites and platforms (remarketing). You can manage or withdraw your consent to these cookies at any time from your browser settings.

Legal basis

Technical cookies are processed on the basis of the legitimate interest in ensuring the website functions properly. Statistical and advertising cookies are processed on the basis of your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

Your rights

As a data subject, you have the right, pursuant to Articles 15-22 of the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679), to access your data and to request its rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and to object to the processing at any time. You can exercise these rights by writing to privacy (at) gtaviani.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali) (www.gpdp.it) if you believe that the processing of your data infringes applicable law.
