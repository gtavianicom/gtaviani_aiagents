📍 Classroom course · Milan · May 10–14, 2027

AI Course for Executives and Managers

Five classroom days in Milan, with Claude: executives decide what to do with AI, teams learn how to use it every day. A program by role, hands-on exercises, a named certificate.

Request Information

The course in numbers

5 days from Monday May 10 to Friday May 14, 2027, in Milan
31h of classroom time: strategy, tools every day, AI agents
Max 10 participants in the classroom, to really work through the exercises
12 months of online platform access, with pre-training from January 11, 2027
Why now

Since February 2, 2025 the AI Act requires companies to provide AI literacy measures for anyone using these tools. The course trains by role and issues a named certificate as evidence.

The week

Monday and Tuesday 10:00–17:00 · Wednesday and Thursday 9:00–17:00 · Friday 9:00–15:00. Lunch break 13:00–14:00.

Monday · StrategyExecutives and department heads only: strategic vs. technical AI, impact on your department, confidential data, GDPR and AI Act.
Tuesday · RulesExecutives and managers together: the 4 levels from prompt to agent mode, assistants for your own role, email and meetings.
Wednesday · DocumentsWord, Excel and slides created by Claude: from report to analysis to presentation, checking numbers and sources.
Thursday · AgentKnowledge base, plugins and skills: a working agent-mode task by the end of the day.
Friday · AdvancedReserved for those attending Tuesday–Thursday (also open to independent professionals): a first agent, then a workforce of 2–3 coordinated agents, then a pilot with metrics to bring back to the company.
