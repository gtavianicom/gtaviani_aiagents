How Much Does E-Commerce Cost Today?

Today, e-commerce rests on two cost centers pulling in different directions.

The external agency

Monthly fee plus a percentage of the media budget. Its business model is simple: the more you spend on ads, the more it earns. Sales are a goal; the media budget is its revenue.

The in-house team

Salaries, contributions, vacation, sick leave, training. And once the person is finally trained, they leave: turnover starts the cycle all over again.

The result

An e-commerce manager costs €40–60,000 in gross annual salary. A full team costs over €100,000/year. And the Christmas peak waits for no one.

FAQ

Does the AI team replace my people?

Yes: the offer replaces the human resources currently dedicated to e-commerce, media and CRM. The company can reassign them to other roles, where human work adds more value (in line with the AI literacy obligation, Art. 4). If today you rely solely on an external agency, the AI team takes its place with a model that has no conflict of interest.

Is the AI team really mine?

Yes. The agents are configured on the company's systems, data and channels, inside a dedicated workspace. GTaviani provides the orchestration and direction; the asset remains the property of the company.

What happens if I cancel?

The plan has a 12-month term with cancellation on request. Upon cancellation, the team's configuration is exported in a standard format, compatible with the proprietary orchestration platform used by GTaviani: no technical lock-in, the data remains the property of the company.

How much does AI usage cost?

It depends heavily on the LLM provider chosen by the retailer (Google, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, etc.): indicatively from $200 to over $1,500/month, depending on the number of active agents and the execution speed. GTaviani consulting works to optimize this cost, minimizing it without compromising performance.

Why only 10 companies?

Because the orchestration is real consulting, not self-service software: every company has a dedicated marketing direction. 10 is the maximum number that guarantees quality.

How long until the team is operational?

Audit and setup take 2–3 weeks. Visibility into performance (the KPIs generated by the AI team) arrives indicatively between the 3rd and 5th month. Companies that start in September reach Black Friday with the team already operational.

Who is needed in the company to orchestrate the AI team?

Usually no one: GTaviani takes care of it. The marketing director or manager remains the human point of contact at the head of the team, or at most one internal intermediation resource.

Does the team manage media channels autonomously?

Absolutely yes, with dedicated AI agents for each channel, guided by the CEC. Each channel agent is connected directly to the retailer's platform account: no markup cost on traffic.

Who handles Content Creation (copy, images, video)?

95% of the work is done by the AI agents. Only human confirmation before publishing remains.

How do CRM and Marketing Automation work?

Here too, AI agents fully connected to the client's CRM and Marketing Automation platforms automate every activity currently carried out by a person. The team defines the customer relationship workflows to grow annual CLV, encouraging more frequent purchases through direct contact channels: email, WhatsApp and others.

Which platforms do the AI agents integrate with?

With almost all modern platforms that expose APIs or support MCP (Model Context Protocol: the open standard that lets an AI agent connect securely and in a structured way to company systems, without one-off custom integrations). Platforms supported include: e-commerce (Shopify, PrestaShop, WooCommerce, Magento), CRM and Marketing Automation (Salesforce, HubSpot, SalesManago, Klaviyo), media channels (Google Ads, Meta Business Suite, TikTok Ads Manager).
