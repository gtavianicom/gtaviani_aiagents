Training funded through interprofessional funds

No pricing here: the economic terms are in the PDF datasheet, sent after the request. This section only explains how the funding works.

Conto Formazione (recommended)

Uses the contributions the company has already paid in (the 0.30% INPS levy). The training provider remains the company's choice, with no minimum amount required and generally fast authorization.

Open calls (larger projects)

A shared fund, ranked by priority, submitted only by entities qualified by the fund. Useful when the project involves several companies or a large number of participants — to be assessed case by case with a partner entity.

How it works, in 4 steps

1 · Check fund and available balance
2 · Agreement with union representatives
3 · Plan filed online, before the courses
4 · Report invoices, attendance records, certificates

GTaviani Consulting provides the program, instructor CV, attendance records, certificates, and invoices ready for reporting. For funded courses, invoicing timing is agreed with the fund.
