FAQ

E-commerce and AI: the basics

What is Agentic Commerce?

It's e-commerce where AI agents, not only people, take actions on behalf of the customer or the merchant: searching, comparing, buying, handling after-sales. Netcomm Forum 2027 dedicates its entire edition to this shift.

What's the difference between a chatbot and an AI Agent?

A chatbot answers questions. An AI Agent has a goal, Conditions of Satisfaction to verify, and can act autonomously across multiple steps, not just converse: this is the distinction at the center of the course's second day.

What is a CDP and why does it matter for AI-first e-commerce?

A Customer Data Platform unifies customer data from multiple sources into a single profile. Without this foundation, AI agents have no consistent data to decide on: it's the starting point of the course's first day.

What changes with the AI Act for e-commerce marketing?

Anyone using AI agents for profiling or automated decisions becomes a deployer under the AI Act, with obligations on transparency (Art. 50) and staff training (Art. 4). The course dedicates a specific module to this.

No: the classroom work uses a test CDP with fictional data. The course explains how to evaluate, set up or improve your own CDP once back at work; it does not require having one already.
