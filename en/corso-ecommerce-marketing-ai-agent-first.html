The program

Two days, two goals: update the method and learn to direct the AI Team. Monday 10:00–17:00, Tuesday 9:00–17:00, lunch break 13:00–14:00.

Monday · E-Commerce Marketing AI first

The Data Driven method updated to 2027: AI agents manage data, platforms and automation workflows.

Data and performanceData Driven in 2027, data and CDP on Google BigQuery, KPI/ROI/ROAS lab.
From CDP to one-to-one actionSegments and automation, the CDP as the agents' decision base, one-to-one lab on test customers.
GDPR and AI ActWhat changes with agents: profiling, AI Act Art. 4 and Art. 50, what to know and what to do.

Tuesday · AI Agents and AI Team

From a single agent to an e-commerce marketing AI Team, directed and coordinated by the e-commerce manager.

AI Agents and the MethodAgent vs chatbot, the 5 components, Conditions of Satisfaction for every task.
The org chart and the typical dayChiefs and operational resources each with one KPI, the 8 o'clock report, approval thresholds.
Lab and governing the teamA test 8 o'clock report, two coordinated agents in AI Space, the team's Conditions of Satisfaction, a first-90-days plan.

The AI Team org chart

E-Commerce Manager

Decides priorities, approves, reads the report.

iCEC · Chief E-Commerce of the AI Team

Breaks goals down into tasks and verifies them.

iCTO

Site and technology: webmaster, performance & UX, SEO & GEO, integrations & data.

iCMO

Channels, content, customers: channel manager, content, social, MA & CRM, customer care.

iCFO

Data and value: historical analysis, projections, media budget, customer value, reporting.

Leaves the classroom with: the printed book, a test KPI dashboard, a one-to-one workflow on the test CDP, the GDPR checklist, the own AI Team org chart, a test 8 o'clock report, two coordinated agents in AI Space, a first-90-days plan and a certificate.
