AI Act & GDPR

Every SMB using an AI agent — even just a chatbot — is already a deployer under the European AI Act: the obligation to provide AI literacy training for staff (Art. 4) is already in force, and from December 2, 2026, the transparency obligations toward customers (Art. 50) take effect — deadlines already set, concrete penalties for those who don't comply.

AI Literacy for Staff

Anyone in the company using AI systems must have an adequate, documented level of competence — an obligation already in force.

Transparency Toward Customers

Anyone interacting with your chatbot or AI agent must know it: disclosure is not optional.

DPA with AI Vendors

Updated Privacy Notice

AI Act & GDPR

AI Act and GDPR for SMBs: how are they interpreted and what are the risks?

Every SMB using an AI agent — even just a chatbot — becomes a deployer under the AI Act, with transparency, documentation and data management obligations that add to those already required by the GDPR. The main risks for those who don't comply are penalties of up to 7% of global turnover, exclusion from tenders and supply contracts requiring AI Act-ready vendors, and direct legal exposure for damages caused by uncontrolled AI systems.

Since when does the AI Act apply to chatbots too?

The obligation to train staff who use AI systems (Art. 4) is already in force. From December 2, 2026, transparency obligations (Art. 50) also take effect: if your chatbot interacts with customers, you're a deployer with disclosure obligations to meet, regardless of your SMB's size.

What happens if I'm not compliant with the AI Act?

You risk penalties of up to 7% of global turnover, exclusion from public and corporate tenders and supply contracts requiring AI Act-ready vendors, and direct legal exposure for damages caused by uncontrolled AI systems.

AI consultants are professionals who support your SMB in adopting artificial intelligence: they don't sell a tool, but design, implement and monitor AI agents over time, speak the language of business, and ensure GDPR and AI Act compliance. An agency develops software to spec and then stops. An AI consultant designs, implements and monitors the agents over time, speaks the language of business, manages the relationship with the platform, and ensures regulatory compliance — ongoing support, not a one-off project.
